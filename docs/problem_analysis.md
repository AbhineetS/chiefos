# Problem Analysis: AI Chief of Staff

## 1. Business Context
Modern CEOs and business executives are inundated with an overwhelming volume of daily tasks—ranging from responding to critical emails, preparing for back-to-back meetings, tracking company OKRs, and synthesizing large amounts of research data. Often, human Chiefs of Staff (CoS) or Executive Assistants manage these streams of information, but they are subject to human bottlenecks and bandwidth constraints. 
An AI-driven virtual Chief of Staff offers a scalable, always-on solution that can digest information asynchronously, coordinate seamlessly across different data streams (calendars, emails, research), and present actionable insights to the executive, freeing up their time for high-leverage strategic decision-making.

## 2. Stakeholders
- **The CEO / Executive**: The primary user whose time is being optimized. They require accurate briefings, prioritized task lists, and safe execution of outbound communications.
- **The Human Chief of Staff / Operations Team**: They act as the "Human-in-the-Loop" for critical actions (e.g., approving drafted emails) and oversee the system's strategic alignment.
- **Internal Team Members**: Secondary stakeholders who receive automated (yet highly accurate) communications, briefs, and follow-ups generated by the AI.

## 3. Problem Statement
Executive leaders suffer from context switching and information overload across disparate tools (email, calendar, internal docs, web research), which slows down decision-making. Existing automation tools are rigid and lack the reasoning capabilities required to connect high-level strategy with daily operations. 
We need to build a resilient, multi-agent AI system that can autonomously break down complex executive requests, dynamically route tasks to specialized agents (email drafting, meeting prep, strategic tracking), and synthesize the results while keeping a human in the loop for critical outbound actions.

## 4. Objectives
1. **Develop a Multi-Agent Architecture**: Implement a centralized `Executive Planner` that can intelligently delegate subtasks to at least 5 specialized agents.
2. **Integrate External Tools**: Equip the agents with tools to fetch calendar data, conduct real-time web research, read local documents, track OKRs, and draft outbound emails.
3. **Ensure Safe Execution (Human-in-the-Loop)**: Implement a hard stop for outbound actions (like sending an email) that requires explicit human approval in the terminal.
4. **Deliver Structured Insights**: Guarantee that the final output from the system is a structured, markdown-formatted executive summary containing key findings and action items.
5. **Implement Robust Error Handling**: Ensure the system handles API rate limits gracefully (using retry loops and exponential backoff) without crashing.
